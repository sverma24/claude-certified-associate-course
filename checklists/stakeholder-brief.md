# One-page brief for a stakeholder (Domain 4, objective 4.5)

1. **What we did:** the tasks Claude helped with.
2. **Time saved:** a table with the task, the time with Claude (including checking), the time by hand, and the **basis**. Keep **measured** and **estimated** apart.
3. **What went wrong:** errors found in review, and what caught them.
4. **What still needs a person:** reviews, decisions about people, money and safety.
5. **Recommendation and next step:** something you can measure, such as a short pilot with before-and-after timings and an error log.

Don't present estimates as measurements, hide errors, or promise that AI will do it all.
