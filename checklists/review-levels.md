# Does a person need to review this? (Domain 2, objective 2.4)

Weigh three things:

| Question | Low | High |
|---|---|---|
| **Stakes:** how bad if it's wrong? | a typo in a team note | money, legal terms, health, safety, accessibility |
| **Audience:** who sees it? | only you | clients, attendees, the public |
| **Reversibility:** can you take it back? | edit and resend | signed, published, paid, promised |

| Level | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **None** | your own working notes | a brainstorm for yourself |
| **Glance** | internal, low stakes, easy to fix | a stand-up reminder |
| **Named review** | anything public, money, legal, health or safety, or a client deliverable | a contract change, an allergy reply, a board budget update, a social post |

**Company policy can raise the bar.** Bramblewick's AI policy (section 4) requires a named reviewer for contract or legal terms, money above $500, health, safety or accessibility, and anything published outside the company. Claude can draft these. It can't be the final check.
