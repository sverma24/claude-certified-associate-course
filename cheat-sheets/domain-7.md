# Domain 7: Troubleshooting and optimization (10%)

Claude Certified Associate Foundations course · cheat sheet

## 7.1 Diagnosing a weak output

Diagnose before you rewrite. Missing context, vague task, wrong feature or model, stale knowledge, overflowing chat, and check the input itself.
Avoid: 1. rewriting everything at once · 2. switching to a bigger model by reflex · 3. fixing the output by hand and never the cause

## 7.2 Adjusting from feedback, with fixes that stick

Feedback that will come back again belongs in the knowledge, the instructions or the Skill. Change it once, test it, and it stays fixed.
Avoid: 1. fixing the same thing by hand every week · 2. changing the Skill and never re-testing · 3. stacking new rules that contradict old ones

## 7.3 Optimizing a workflow for time, cost and quality

Optimize with projects, Skills, the right model and the lightest feature. Keep the reviews that protect people, and measure before and after.
Avoid: 1. the biggest model and Research for everything · 2. cutting the review step to save time · 3. claiming savings you never measured
