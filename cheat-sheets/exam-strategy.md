## 5.0 Part 5: Exam strategy



## X1 How the questions are written

Read the question line first, name the domain, strike the clearly wrong, and let the scenario's constraint decide. Multiple response always tells you how many.

## X2 Confusion pairs

Project for ongoing work, Skill for a reusable procedure, memory for what's learned. Search is quick, Research is deep. Anonymize before, not after. Claude's own check is never the final check.

## X3 Why the safe-but-useful answer wins

Wrong answers send as-is, ban AI, self-check, or reach for the biggest model. The right answer keeps the value and adds the right control.

## X4 Exam day

Matching ID, a clear desk, two minutes an item with a flag-and-return plan, and check the multiple-response counts at the end. Renew on time, it's free.

## X5 What's changed since the guide

Names and buttons change, the principles don't. Answer by role and by principle, and check what's new before you sit the exam.

## X6 A two-weekend study plan

Over two weekends, do the foundations and the biggest domain first, then the rest and the capstone, then two practice exams with a review in between.
