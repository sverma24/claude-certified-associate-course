# Domain 4: Workflow integration and solution design (16%)

Claude Certified Associate Foundations course · cheat sheet

## 4.1 From a messy request to clear requirements

Turn messy requests into requirements, must-haves, nice-to-haves and questions. Weigh every idea on value, risk and how often it's used.
Avoid: 1. starting work from the raw email · 2. treating every wish as a must-have · 3. adopting an idea because it sounds modern

## 4.2 Research, planning and fixing a slow process

Research gives you a starting point with graded sources. For a slow process, map it, find the bottleneck, and fix that first.
Avoid: 1. treating a research report as the decision · 2. automating a broken process as it is · 3. guessing the time saved instead of measuring

## 4.3 Building through feedback, and knowing when to hand off

Build in rounds, one change at a time, and test with the people who'll use it. When it needs logins, live company data or security, write a brief and hand it off.
Avoid: 1. building past the edge and hoping · 2. collecting feedback but changing everything at once · 3. a hand-off with no brief

## 4.4 Reshaping a workflow: augment or redesign

Augment helps with a step. Redesign changes the steps. Either way, build the human review into the design.
Avoid: 1. redesigning with no review step · 2. automating the wrong step · 3. not telling the people whose work changes

## 4.5 Explaining value and limits to stakeholders

Show the value with honest numbers, measured kept apart from estimated. Name the limits and what still needs a person, and recommend a next step you can measure.
Avoid: 1. presenting estimates as measurements · 2. hiding the errors · 3. 'AI will do it all' instead of what still needs a person
