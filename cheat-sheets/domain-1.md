# Domain 1: Prompting and task execution (14%)

Claude Certified Associate Foundations course · cheat sheet

## 1.1 Writing prompts that carry what Claude needs

A good prompt is a good handover. Task, context, audience, format, constraints, examples, and what done means.
Avoid: 1. vague nouns ('the caterers', 'the event') · 2. leaving out the deadline or the reader · 3. no 'done means', so no way to check

## 1.2 Breaking a big job into steps

Big jobs go better in steps. Plan first, supply what's missing, then run and check one step at a time.
Avoid: 1. asking for everything in one prompt · 2. not checking between steps · 3. letting Claude skip missing inputs

## 1.3 Improving a result, one change at a time

Diagnose, change one thing, then re-check the whole output. Stop when it does its job, and count anything Claude counts.
Avoid: 1. rewriting the whole prompt every round · 2. changing three things at once · 3. checking only the part you changed

## 1.4 Choosing the approach for the type of task

Match the approach to the task. Analysis needs criteria, research needs graded sources, drafting needs facts, and brainstorming needs range before judgment.
Avoid: 1. asking for 'the best 3 ideas' straight away · 2. treating a research answer as one exact figure · 3. a draft with no audience
