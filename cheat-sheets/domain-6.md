# Domain 6: Governance, risk and responsible use (15%)

Claude Certified Associate Foundations course · cheat sheet

## 6.1 Appropriate and inappropriate use

Claude helps people decide. It doesn't decide about people, and it doesn't give final answers on health or safety.
Avoid: 1. letting Claude make people decisions · 2. reassuring answers on health or safety · 3. refusing to use Claude at all, when it could help safely

## 6.2 Data sensitivity, privacy and anonymizing

Classify, minimize, anonymize. Telling Claude not to keep something isn't protection. Removing it before upload is.
Avoid: 1. 'I'll tell Claude not to keep it' · 2. uploading all columns when you need three · 3. anonymizing so badly you lose the duplicates

## 6.3 Following your organization's AI policy

Know your policy's approved tools, data rules, approvals and review steps. When a request breaks it, offer the compliant route, and report mistakes the same day.
Avoid: 1. a flat 'no' with no alternative · 2. installing outside Skills or connectors without approval · 3. using a personal account 'just this once'

## 6.4 Ethics: fairness, openness and accountability

Check for fairness, be open about AI use, put a name on every output, respect others' work, and keep the skills to judge what Claude gives you.
Avoid: 1. publishing AI work with no owner · 2. AI images passed off as real · 3. trusting outputs about people without a fairness check
