name: bramblewick-proposal-style
description: Use when drafting a client proposal or price quote for Bramblewick Events. Applies our house structure, pricing rules and tone.
---

Write every Bramblewick client proposal in this order:
Step 1. One sentence: what we will deliver, for whom, and the event date.
Step 2. "What you told us": three bullets restating the client's goals in their words.
Step 3. "What we'll do": a table with Service, What's included, Price.
Step 4. "Price": the total, a line saying prices exclude tax, and the name of the price list used.
Step 5. "Next step": one action with a date.

Rules:
- Price only from the Bramblewick service price list in the project. Show the working for any per-attendee fee and apply its minimum.
- If a price you need is missing, write [price needed] instead of guessing.
- No exclamation marks. Never use "world-class", "seamless", "synergy", "game-changing" or "cutting-edge".
- Keep it under 300 words.
- End with [Planner name] and [direct line].
- State whether each price includes service and tax, as the brand guide requires. If the price list doesn't say, write [service charge: to confirm].
